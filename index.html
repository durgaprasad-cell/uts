THE MISSION

A better signal for
moving cities.

Public transport data is often delayed, fragmented, or too coarse to describe what is happening on the ground. UTSP gives commuters a simple way to report the moment as they experience it.

That creates a more human, more local intelligence layer: one that can help communities navigate today and help mobility teams plan tomorrow.
